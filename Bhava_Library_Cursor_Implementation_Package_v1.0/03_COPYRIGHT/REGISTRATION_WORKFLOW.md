# U.S. Copyright Registration Workflow

This is a tracking and preparation workflow, not a substitute for legal advice.

## Automatic protection

Copyright protection exists automatically when an original work is fixed. Formal U.S. registration is a separate step that provides additional legal benefits.

## Publication-status gate

Before selecting a registration application, record whether the work is:

- unpublished;
- published;
- first published online;
- first published in print;
- distributed only to a limited review group;
- previously published in another version.

Do not guess. Publication status affects the correct registration path.

## Existing unpublished stories

The U.S. Copyright Office currently permits up to ten eligible unpublished works in one Group of Unpublished Works application when the eligibility rules are met. Each work must be uploaded as a separate file.

This may be relevant to the nine existing Bhāva stories only if:

- all remain unpublished under the applicable definition;
- authorship/claimant requirements are met;
- the same author/coauthor conditions are met;
- no work was already published;
- the correct group application is selected.

If any story has already been publicly distributed, do not include it in an unpublished group without professional review.

## Registration ledger

Maintain:

```text

```

## Deposit preparation

- freeze the submitted version;
- calculate SHA-256;
- retain the exact submitted file;
- record the metadata used;
- retain the receipt and correspondence;
- never overwrite the deposit file.

## Recommended title-level strategy

For finished comics and illustrated books, use a title-level registration plan unless counsel confirms a valid group option. A comic can combine literary and visual authorship and may involve contractors or commissioned artwork; ownership documentation must be complete first.

## Privacy note

Copyright registration information becomes a public record. Do not insert a private home address or private personal data without understanding the public-record consequence. Use the public contact information approved by the owner and obtain professional guidance for legal-name and address fields.

## Review cadence

Review the official Copyright Office requirements and fees immediately before filing. Store the access date and official source link in the registration record.
