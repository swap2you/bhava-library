# Copyright and Publication Policy

This is operational guidance, not legal advice.

## Two entirely different classes of files

### A. Third-party reference originals

Examples:

- Ministry PDFs;
- externally authored curricula;
- existing comics;
- audio;
- images;
- Office files;
- archives.

Rules:

- preserve unchanged;
- retain all embedded notices and metadata;
- do not add a Swarna Gauranga Das or Dauji Publication copyright notice;
- do not remove a watermark;
- do not rewrite metadata to claim ownership;
- do not publish through Bhāva;
- do not place in `exports/bhava-ready`;
- show a private UI warning:
  `REFERENCE ORIGINAL — RIGHTS REMAIN WITH THE ORIGINAL CREATOR/PUBLISHER`.

### B. Original Bhāva/Dauji works

Examples:

- newly written stories;
- newly designed comics;
- original illustrations;
- original printables;
- teacher guides;
- original narration;
- original layouts and compilations where protectable.

Public attribution:

- Copyright owner: Swarna Gauranga Das
- Publisher: Dauji Publication
- Project: Bhāva
- Location: Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, USA
- Contact: SwarnaGaurangaDas@gmail.com

## Copyright protection versus notice

An original work is protected when it is fixed in a tangible medium. Adding a footer does not create copyright, and omitting a footer does not necessarily remove copyright.

A notice is still recommended. For a visually perceptible work it normally includes:

1. ©;
2. year of first publication;
3. owner name.

For a sound recording, use ℗ for the sound-recording claim. A recording may also contain a separate underlying literary or musical work.

## Ownership line

Until a legal entity or documented transfer changes ownership, use:

`Copyright © YYYY Swarna Gauranga Das. All rights reserved.`

Use the publisher separately:

`Published by Dauji Publication, a Bhāva Project publication.`

Do not list Dauji Publication as the copyright owner merely because it is the imprint, unless ownership has legally been assigned to that entity.

## Evidence before publication

Every original work must have:

- work ID;
- exact title;
- version;
- creation date;
- contributors;
- authorship roles;
- source dossier;
- asset provenance;
- font licenses;
- AI-use record;
- file hashes;
- approval record;
- first-publication date;
- published formats and territories;
- registration status.

## Source-material rule

Common scriptural subjects and historical facts may inform new work, but a final product must not copy protected modern expression.

Do not carry over:

- distinctive wording;
- invented dialogue;
- illustration;
- character design;
- panel composition;
- page sequence;
- worksheets;
- translation;
- narration;
- cover design;
- typography treatment.

Create from a bona fide source dossier and maintain independent-creation evidence.

## Internal drafts

Draft notice:

`DRAFT — NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION`
`Copyright © YYYY Swarna Gauranga Das.`

## Preview copies

Use an unobtrusive preview watermark only for previews:

`PREVIEW — NOT FOR REDISTRIBUTION`

Do not watermark every page of the final consumer book.

## Printables and worksheets

Place a small footer on every printable page because pages are commonly separated:

`© YYYY Swarna Gauranga Das · Dauji Publication · Bhāva Project · License terms at [future URL]`

The license phrase must match the actual sale/subscription license.

## Books and comics

Use:

- copyright page;
- metadata;
- optional small colophon;
- back-cover publisher identity.

Do not repeat a large watermark on every page.

## Audio

Metadata and packaging should distinguish:

- literary/script claim: ©;
- sound-recording claim: ℗.

Example:

`Text © YYYY Swarna Gauranga Das.`
`Sound recording ℗ YYYY Swarna Gauranga Das.`
`Published by Dauji Publication, a Bhāva Project publication.`

## Website

Use a site-level footer for the platform, but maintain per-work first-publication years in the work manifest.

## Corrections

Never silently replace a released file. Create a new version, new hash, correction note, and updated publication record.
